1. Overview and Scope

This Refund and Cancellation Policy applies to freelance software development, engineering consultations, and contracted technical services provided by Abhinash Maddeshiya ("Developer", "I", "me").

2. Nature of Software Services

Software development and consulting are knowledge-based services tailored to specific client specifications. Prior to commencing any paid engagement, both parties agree to a written Statement of Work (SOW) defining:

  • Technical scope, milestones, and deliverables.
  • Estimated timelines and communication schedules.
  • Transparent fee structures with zero hidden costs.

3. Milestone Payments and Refund Eligibility

To ensure mutual fairness, consulting contracts are structured around discrete development milestones:

  • Pre-Commencement Refunds: If an upfront deposit is paid and the client cancels the engagement before any engineering work or architectural planning has begun, a 100% refund of the deposit is granted (minus any third-party payment processing fees).
  • In-Progress Milestones: If an engagement is terminated mid-milestone, billing is calculated pro-rata based solely on work completed up to the cancellation notice. Any unworked balance from the advance deposit is refunded within 7 business days.
  • Completed & Approved Milestones: Once a specific milestone has been delivered, reviewed, and approved by the client, payments associated with that milestone are non-refundable as the intellectual labor has been performed.

4. Dispute Resolution and Quality Guarantee

If delivered code does not meet the technical requirements agreed upon in the SOW, I provide a complimentary 14-day defect resolution window to rectify verified bugs or non-conformances at zero additional charge.

5. Open-Source Projects
