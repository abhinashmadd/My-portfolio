Cookie & Storage Policy

Full transparency on browser storage technologies and your privacy choices

1. What Are Cookies and Web Storage?

Cookies and modern browser storage technologies (such as localStorage and sessionStorage) are small text files or key-value entries stored directly within your web browser. They allow websites to remember user preferences, maintain authenticated sessions, and ensure basic functional stability.

2. Our Strict Privacy Approach

This developer portfolio adheres to strict data minimization principles:

  • No Third-Party Advertising Cookies: I do not host ad networks, marketing pixels, or third-party behavioral trackers.
  • No User Profiling: Your browsing activity across other websites is never tracked, recorded, or monitored.
  • Strictly Necessary and Functional Only: Storage is utilized exclusively to deliver essential user experience features (such as maintaining your chosen Dark/Light theme).

3. Inventory of Storage Keys Used

Below is the exhaustive inventory of browser storage items utilized across this website:

Item Key Type Purpose Duration
am_portfolio_theme localStorage Stores your chosen visual theme preference (Dark Mode or Light Mode). Persistent (until cleared)
am_cookie_consent localStorage Records your cookie preference selection so the consent banner is not shown repeatedly. Persistent (12 months)
am_admin_session sessionStorage Stores the cryptographically signed HMAC token for authenticated administrator dashboard sessions. Session only (cleared on tab close)
am_admin_role sessionStorage Caches active dashboard role permissions (Lead Administrator, Recruiter Reviewer, or Guest). Session only (cleared on tab close)

4. Third-Party Resource Audit

To ensure transparency, here is the audit of all external resources linked or referenced by this site:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com): Used to serve modern typography (Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, JetBrains Mono). Fonts are loaded without setting user tracking cookies.
  • Devicons / jsDelivr CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net): Used to display vector developer technology logos under the MIT License. No marketing tracking is performed.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): Used as an email forwarding proxy when a visitor voluntarily submits the contact form. No persistent cookies are injected during passive page browsing.
  • Firebase Firestore (gstatic.com): Optional client-side database connector initialized for real-time contact sync on static hosting environments. It does not set marketing cookies.

5. Managing and Clearing Your Preferences

You have complete control over your browser storage:

  • Consent Banner: You can click "Decline Non-Essential" on our consent banner at any time to reject optional storage.
  • Browser Settings: You can inspect, block, or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings:
    • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
    • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
    • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
    • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions
